The check of a generated plan after a generate round: the compliance policies,
Rego policies evaluated by OPA (a general one and one per provider of the
module), then an independent, second LLM agent that
checks the plan against the organization's compliance rules. Failing
findings of either, or a policy that cannot be evaluated —
and, optionally, a high-impact verdict — lock the session until a panel
editor unlocks it or a later generate round passes clean.
